About the project

Old songs, local stories,
one living culture.

Pahadidhun is a frontend-only project inspired by the culture of Far-Western Nepal. It presents local traditions, heritage, and old Deuda songs in a warm digital experience. The music experience is organised around the time of day, so different songs can play for different moments without needing a backend.

01

Far-Western culture

The project celebrates the region's traditions, heritage, local identity, and cultural stories.

02

Old Deuda songs

A curated collection of traditional Deuda songs gives the experience its cultural and musical character.

03

Time-based music

Frontend JavaScript uses the time of day to select the appropriate song or playlist for the visitor.

04

No backend required

The experience is built with static frontend content and browser-side logic, so it works without a server or database.
